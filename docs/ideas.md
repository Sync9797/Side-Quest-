# Ideas

Ideas for later. None of these are planned yet.

## Endgame

- **Star dungeons:** from star 1, a rare 6-star "Mythic" dungeon appears on the
  map, with tougher rooms and champions and a star-only prize (cosmetic or a
  title, not raw power).
- **Reforging:** spend weapon parts or armor scrap to reroll a piece of gear's
  bonus effects. Gives spare parts a use and keeps a better roll to chase.
- **Weekly champion:** one named boss each week with a scoring rule (fewest
  turns, no healing, a single monster). You compete with your own best for a
  badge.
- **Collection goals:** Journal pages for shinies and perfect-gene monsters,
  with titles for completing them.

- **Spear and war hammer:** two more exotic weapons that join at level 50,
  hard to master like the axe, bow and scythe, each with a trade-off. Spear:
  strikes first and pierces Defense, but deals less damage. War hammer: huge
  hits that can stun, but slow and inaccurate.

## Dungeons and torches

- **Scout the champion:** spend a torch before the champion fight to see their
  team, then pick your lineup.
- **Escape torch:** spend 2 torches to slip out mid-fight and keep everything
  you found, instead of losing half when your team falls.
- **Torch records:** a best record per star rating for the deepest dungeon
  cleared with the fewest torches, so clever play counts, not just walking a
  lot.

## Real life

- **Habit streaks:** repeating to-dos (gym, reading) build a streak, and each
  day kept adds a small bonus. Missing a day only drops the streak a step, so
  one off day doesn't wipe weeks of progress.
- **Quest difficulty tags:** quick, normal or big to-dos with rewards to match,
  confirmed when you complete them and still under the daily fair-use limits.

## Map

- **District control:** clearing an area's thugs keeps it safe for a few days
  (a soft color on the map) and gives a small trader discount there.
- **Roaming legend:** one strong monster wanders the city each week, moving
  daily; shrines give clues to where it is.

## Social and polish

- **Weekly recap card:** every Sunday, a shareable card with steps, quests,
  phone-free hours, monsters tamed and the best fight of the week.

## Pacing

- **Spell rank and champion limits** are in (3.13). If heroes still max out too
  fast, slow the late XP curve or tie star ranks to a longer climb.
